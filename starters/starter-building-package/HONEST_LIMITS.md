# Honest Limits

Read this before you treat the starter as a finished AI Campus.

## Hatch is multi-step

Copying the folder is not the finish line. You still fill One-time Setup, replace placeholders, rename seats, write Mission, and (optionally) wire Town Hall. Expect a working afternoon, not a one-click install.

## Obsidian is recommended, not required

This release was written in Obsidian, which is free for personal use. Download it from https://obsidian.md/download if you want it. Any folder of Markdown files works. You do not need a paid plugin stack. See `How to open these notes.md`.

## Personas are shells

Architect and Director files are starting shapes. Voice, judgment, and Lessons grow from use. Do not expect a finished character on day one.

## No silent email or calendar control

Nothing in this package grants automatic control of your real mail, calendar, or phone. Access Boundaries require {{OWNER}} approval before those surfaces are touched. Town Hall "mail" here means Markdown packets between vault folders.

## Review rhythm is yours

The boot loop and When I Go Home lists help habit. They do not run themselves. Weekly review, clearing Inbox, and naming Cases stay human work.

## Solo vs campus

A solo Building does not need Town Hall boxes, a campus operations secretary, or a vault architect on day one. Those seats belong to a multi-Building campus layer. Consequence mail can wait until that layer exists.

## Influence, not brand

If you already practice Getting Things Done (David Allen), some ideas will feel familiar (capture, next actions, review). Do not brand this Building as GTD. Keep official tickets wherever you already keep them; do not duplicate them onto Case Cover Sheets.
