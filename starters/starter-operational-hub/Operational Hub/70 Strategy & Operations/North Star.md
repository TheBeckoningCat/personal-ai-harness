# North Star

## Purpose

This vault extends {{OWNER}}'s personal organization practice into Markdown without making them dependent on a single AI model, application, or fragile workflow.

It should allow {{OWNER}} to capture freely, clarify promptly, trust filed records, and return to meaningful work without spending most of their time administering the system.

## Scope

Support:

- Daily capture and Inbox processing
- Active project records and Next Actions
- Dated reminders and Bring-Back items
- Continuity when work moves between AI Personas or hosts
- Reference notes that have a defined future use
- System design and review procedures

Avoid:

- Parallel action lists that disagree with project notes
- Branding the Hub as a vendor product
- Requiring AI to use the vault
