# Areas and Contexts

**Status:** #status/active · V1 ratified 2026-08-06  
**Architect:** Vault Architect · **Vocab owner:** Owner · **Maintainer:** Operations Secretary  

`20 Areas & Contexts` holds the lists. This file is how they work.

## Rule

- **Areas** = one primary life-responsibility label on a **project**. Approved list only: [[20 Areas & Contexts/Areas of Life]].
- **Contexts** = where / how / with whom on a **Next Action** line only. Approved list: [[20 Areas & Contexts/Contexts]]. Not on ordinary Planned lines. A Context on Planned is allowed only next to `NEXT`, as instructions for Operations Secretary’s move. Not a second project tree.
- **Priority** is a separate axis: [[Project Priority]]. Not an Area. Not a Context.
- Leave `area` blank if it is unclear. Do not invent a new Area or Context during inbox processing. Operations Secretary may propose; Owner approves.

## Who

| Who | Does |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Approves additions and renames |
| **Operations Secretary** | Spelling, duplicates, proposals. Uses the list on new and touched projects. No mass rewrite of the library. |
| **Vault Architect** | Folder role and this SOP. Not the vocabulary. |

## Do not

- Use this folder as a project store or dashboard.
- Put contexts on the project as a whole.
- Point working notes at Scratch Pad for this rule. The rule lives here.
