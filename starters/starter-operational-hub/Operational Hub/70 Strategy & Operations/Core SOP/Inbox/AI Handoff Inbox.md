This Inbox receives packets from AI collaborators working outside the vault. Operations Secretary processes the packets into GTD. Outside collaborators may propose actions and projects, but Operations Secretary determines the official GTD location.

## Purpose

Outside collaborators such as Domain Specialist, John, Domain Specialist, and outside personal assistant use a short packet to preserve useful results from their conversations. Operations Secretary decides whether each result belongs in a project, Next Actions, Waiting For, Resources, Someday-Maybe, or nowhere in GTD.

## How Owner Uses It

1. Paste one complete packet under **Unprocessed Session Updates** or **Unprocessed Full Handoffs**.
2. Prefer a Session Update that covers only the current work session. Use a Full Handoff for a first comprehensive transfer or when Owner specifically requests one.
3. Do not include credentials, account numbers, multifactor authentication codes, or unneeded private email.
4. Ask Operations Secretary to process the packet, or leave it for her normal Inbox review.

## How Outside Collaborators Use It

- Send a Session Update after a useful work period. Send a Full Handoff only when Owner requests one.
- Identify proposed Next Actions and Project Candidates clearly. Do not describe them as approved GTD decisions.
- Operations Secretary and Vault Architect do not use this Inbox for routine internal coordination. They use their Mail Boxes and Scratch Pads.
- Use this Inbox only when Operations Secretary must process substantive GTD information from outside the vault.

## What Operations Secretary Does

1. Read the packet without reconstructing or reprocessing the entire conversation.
2. Record durable facts in the appropriate project or create a project when the packet establishes a distinct active outcome.
3. Archive the packet under `30 Resources/AI Handoff Archive/` with a dated filename.
4. Move its entry from Unprocessed to **Processed Handoffs**, recording the date, source, and official destination.
5. Remove the processed packet from the unprocessed section.

Operations Secretary may merge, revise, or reject proposed GTD actions when the official records or the Owner's current instructions require a different result.
