# Project Priority (v1)

**Status:** #status/active · ratified Owner 2026-08-09 · Architect: Vault Architect  
**Scope:** Project-level frontmatter `priority` only. **Not** next-action priority.

Authoritative priority scale for [[Templates/Project Template]], [[70 Strategy & Operations/Projects.base|Projects Base]], and the Operations Secretary's project creation and review work.

---

## Legend

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `1A` | High importance / **lower** effort or complexity |
| `1B` | High importance / **higher** effort or complexity |
| `2A` | Lower importance / **lower** effort or complexity |
| `2B` | Lower importance / **higher** effort or complexity |
| *(blank)* | Not yet assessed, or Operations Secretary/Owner confidence is low — **do not invent** |

Allowed values only: `1A` · `1B` · `2A` · `2B` · blank.  
No free-text priority. No intermediate scores.

Natural sort order (also lexicographic): **1A → 1B → 2A → 2B** → blank.

---

## Rules

1. **Importance × effort/complexity** — not urgency. Urgency stays on due dates, [[Calendar/Calendar]], [[Calendar/Bring-Back Index]], or other time-sensitive handling.
2. **Project-level only.** Next actions use [[20 Areas & Contexts/Contexts]] and time markers; do not put 1A–2B on action lines.
3. **Operations Secretary** may assign a best-effort value on create/process/review from Daily, journals, known commitments, risk, and project significance.
4. **Owner** overrides at any time.
5. If confidence is low → **leave blank** rather than force a ranking.
6. **No mass migration.** Apply to new projects and to touched/reviewed projects over time.
