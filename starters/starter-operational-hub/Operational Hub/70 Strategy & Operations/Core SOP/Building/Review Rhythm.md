# Review Rhythm

## Daily
- Complete morning, midday, or evening Daily reviews according to [[Daily Note Standard]].
- Copy the Owner's two morning priorities into Must Do. Add only time-sensitive commitments, and move handled Capture into Processed.
- Process Inbox as needed. Check Calendar and only the Due today and Coming soon sections of Bring-Back. Do not copy the full Index into Daily.
- Review journal reflection according to [[Journal Review Standard]], separately from the Daily overview.

## Evening
- Complete an evening Daily review when Owner asks: finish moving handled Capture into Processed and remove stale today-only information.
- Do not turn Daily into a retrospective (that is Daily Journal).
- Record durable outcomes in projects, Resources, Calendar, Bring-Back, or Someday-Maybe as appropriate.
- Record meaningful AI work in the Work Log. Update the Session Brief only when orientation changes. Do not place the Operations Secretary's processing transcript in Daily.

## Weekly
- Review [[70 Strategy & Operations/Projects.base|Projects Base]] per [[Active Projects Map]].
- Check stale projects and waiting-for items.
- Move completed projects toward archive.
- Review whether the system felt easier or heavier.

## As Needed
- Big capture day: use [[Signal Preservation Protocol]].
- Current app support: use [[Main Lobby/README]] before writing durable instructions.
- Naming / structure changes: decide here before renaming folders.
- Bring-Back: follow v2 only — Owner-directed dates; drain any **Migration — Rehome Queue**; never rebuild an undated TBD section.
