# Main Lobby

Entry point for the Operational Hub (The Brain). The Operations Secretary manages capture, processing, and review. The Vault Architect manages vault structure. Begin the boot sequence here, then follow the instructions for your office.

The vault is a digital extension of {{OWNER}}'s paper or prior personal organization system. Staff may work in the vault, but the vault must remain usable without a particular AI host.
