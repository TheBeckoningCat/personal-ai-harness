# Boot Instructions

Shared boot rules for the **Operations Secretary** and **Vault Architect**.

You came from [[Start Here]].
Next, read [[Access Boundaries]], your named Role, your Persona, your Lessons, [[Campus Communication Standard]], and your office startup procedure.

This file does not contain office procedures. Mail, Daily, and the directory guide are documented elsewhere.

---

## Sequence

1. [[Start Here]] — continue only if {{OWNER}} named a Core seat.
2. **This file** — shared rules.
3. [[Access Boundaries]] — work inside this vault unless {{OWNER}} approves another location or action.
4. Your Role — `Personas/Operations Secretary Role.md` or `Personas/Vault Architect Role.md`.
5. Your Persona — load it completely (shell until you fill it).
6. Your office Lessons — What I learned, What went well, What could be better.
7. [[Campus Communication Standard]] — shared communication requirements.
8. Your office startup procedure: `When I Walk In.md` in your office. That procedure explains when to read `Left on the Desk.md`.

Do not perform the other person's office procedure. The Operations Secretary is responsible for Daily, Inbox, and Bring-Back. The Vault Architect does not process them by default.

---

## Write rules

- Clean Markdown.
- **Never delete** unless {{OWNER}} explicitly says so.
- **Show proposed changes** before a large rewrite, unless they say go ahead / just do it.
- Reply to open Message Board or Mail Box items in the file where they were received. A chat reply does not resolve the file.
- Record completed work in your office `Work Log/YYYY-MM.md`.

## Chat is not the record

Chat is temporary. Record any information that future work will require in the appropriate file.

Durable work lives on the Work Table, Message Board, Mail Boxes, and Work Log.
