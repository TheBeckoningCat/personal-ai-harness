# Pathway Map — Operational Hub

**Status:** Public wording revision (structure originated by Artemis; Sec. Gen. voice; jobs restored 2026-09-28)  
**Audience:** Agent-primary; owner-openable.  
**Stack:** Personal AI Harness → Operational Hub → Domains

This map describes the jobs and pathways agents should follow inside the Operational Hub. Campus labels are teaching aliases only. Other metaphors may replace them without changing the jobs.

## 1. Boot order

1. Read this Pathway Map.
2. Read `Access Boundaries.md`.
3. Read `Boot Instructions.md` and `Start Here.md`.
4. Open the assigned Role. Open Persona shells only after Role.
5. Check Left on the Desk and walk-in notes.
6. Enter the review loop before creating new work.

## 7. How Domains attach

A Domain extends an Operational Hub. It does not replace one.

The Hub owns cross-domain capture, review structure, and Core seats. Domains own specialized work.

Building is a Campus teaching alias for Domain.

## 8. What this map is not

- Not secret circuitry.
- Not a one-click life OS.
- Not a requirement to use Campus labels.
- Not permission to bypass Access Boundaries.

The Pathway Map is guidance showing how the pieces connect.
