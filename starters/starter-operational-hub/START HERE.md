## What you will walk (in this order — one beat at a time)

Do not rush. Finish each beat before opening the next. Ask **one** clear question when a human decision is needed.

### 1. What this package is

Explain in plain words:

- A **Personal AI Harness** is a way to organize notes and AI help around **your** life — not a single app vendor lock-in.
- This zip is the **Operational Hub** starter: your life-level organization desk (capture, projects, next actions, review).
- A **Domain** is a specialized area (health, work, home, study, and so on). In AI Campus teaching language, Domains are often shown as **Buildings**. This package is **not** a Building starter. Buildings come later, only if an area truly needs its own vault.

Honest limits (state calmly):

- This is **not** a one-click finished AI Campus.
- Nothing here silently runs email or calendar for them.
- Markdown files are the store of record. Chat is temporary.

### 2. How to view the Hub (and later a Domain)

Many people have never opened a `.md` file. Explain in plain words, then ask **one** question.

**What a `.md` file is:** a text note with a `.md` ending. Headings and lists are ordinary words. The Hub is a folder of those notes. A Domain (Building) is the same kind of folder for one area of life. They can read either without learning a programming language.

**What we use for this release:** this starter was written and used in **Obsidian**, free for personal use. They do not have to install it.

**If they want Obsidian, you must help them reach the real site.** Do not invent a download URL. Give this exact address as a real link if your chat can:

https://obsidian.md/download

Home page if they want to look around first: https://obsidian.md

If you cannot post a clickable link, tell them: open a web browser, type **obsidian.md/download**, and pick Windows, Mac, or Linux. Phone apps are listed on that same page. Do not send them to a random search result or a third-party store page you guessed.

They do not need a paid Obsidian account, Sync, Publish, or any plugin.

Then, if they installed it: **Open folder as vault**, and choose the `Operational Hub` folder (the one with `00 Inbox` and `Main Lobby`). For a Domain later, choose the `Building` folder (the one with `Main Lobby`, `Cases`, and `Inbox`). Details are in `How to open these notes.md`.

**If they do not want Obsidian:** Finder, File Explorer, or Files, then any text editor (TextEdit, Notepad, Notes). Links between notes are nicer in Obsidian; the words are the same.

Ask once: “Do you already have a way to open these notes, or would you like help installing Obsidian?” Wait. Do not require a paid plugin stack. Do not require a Mac. If they ask about a double-click starter, wait for beat 8. This package does not include runnable `.command` or `.bat` files.
